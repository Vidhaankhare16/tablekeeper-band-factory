## Readings (ambiguous or silent text)
- **P1** A wrong JSON *type* in a policy or series field (string/list/object for an integer, etc.) may be 400 `malformed_request` (§5) or 422 `validation_failed` (policy
  text "Invalid policy is 422"); both are accepted, never a 2xx and never a state change. Booleans and non-integral numbers are strictly 422 ("booleans are not integers", "Invalid values, including booleans, give 422").
- **P2** "A no-op amendment … still requires a confirmed, editable booking": cancelled ⇒ 409 `reservation_cancelled`; inside the accepted cutoff ⇒ 409 `cutoff_passed`.
- **P3** `expected_revision` invalid type/range (0, negative, string, bool, float, list, object) ⇒ 422 `validation_failed`; a non-owner still gets 404 (never 409) even with a stale revision.
- **P4** Series: missing `count` or `interval_weeks` ⇒ 422; missing `anchor_reference` ⇒ 422; wrong-type fields 400|422 (P1). Error order *between* different failure kinds (e.g. unknown anchor vs invalid count) is not asserted.
- **P5** History `at` is any RFC 3339 timestamp with an offset; imported/seeded records have at least a contiguous history (the *content* of an imported record's first entry is not asserted beyond seq/revision).
- **P6** `GET /reservations/{ref}/history|decision` are 404 for everyone but the owner including managers and unauthenticated callers; `POST /series` and `GET /series/{id}` are the only owner-only series endpoints.
- **P7** Replays of keys created by an earlier stage return that stage's original body verbatim (no `revision`/`accepted_terms`/`table_ids` added).
- **P8 Restaurant revision is unobservable.** The text says adoption increments the restaurant revision once and a batch increments it once, but no endpoint, header or field exposes it
  (restaurant detail "still returns its original fixture configuration"; policies carry only `policy_version`). It is therefore **not asserted**; the observable consequences (revisions of
  reservations and series, histories, exception flags, replays, atomicity) are.
- Not asserted: relative order of two simultaneous field faults; an exact-second cutoff boundary; the textual content of messages.
